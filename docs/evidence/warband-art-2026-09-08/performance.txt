Local native performance comparison, 2026-09-08

Fixture: tools.perf_warband.battle, 150 spawned soldiers plus starting workers.
Units/images fully pre-rendered; 12 settling frames, then 360 frames paced at
30 FPS with tools.native_frames.tick. Pacing excluded from measured Game.tick.
FrameTimer wraps world.step, view.sync, scene.draw, backend.end_frame.
No profiler or tracemalloc. Both comparisons ended with 136 units alive.
Baseline loads warband/textures.py and warband/view.py from b7f8eb6^; current
loads the art overhaul. Both use the same other source and seeded fixture.

Baseline: all 360 frames p50 8.3 ms, p95 17.5 ms, max 39.5 ms.
Current:  all 360 frames p50 8.9 ms, p95 18.5 ms, max 42.0 ms.

Late 120 frames:
Baseline: p50 7.92 ms, p95 14.56 ms, max 36.77 ms.
Current:  p50 8.45 ms, p95 18.38 ms, max 36.51 ms.

Mean component times over all 360 frames:
                  baseline   current
backend.end_frame  4.89 ms    5.09 ms
world.step         3.49 ms    3.83 ms  (120 fixed simulation steps)

Earlier 180-frame current-only check (148 units alive): p50 7.25 ms,
p95 16.68 ms, max 31.84 ms. The 10_battle.png capture is from this run.

The art is pre-rendered; it adds no per-frame mesh growth. This short local
comparison shows a modest mean rendering increase but does NOT establish the
project's W10 p95 <16 ms gate. That target remains unmet in this current run.
These are bounded checks, not a long-match or multiplayer performance soak.
